# Decent group-head button artwork

Original artwork: Decent Espresso, https://decentespresso.com/docs/group_head_icons

The official page provides the artwork for use in skins. The three SVGs use the original Bézier outlines from `GH ICONS 8 MM THICKNESS.ai`, including its finished steam-cloud contour. They have only been centered, uniformly scaled, and converted from the source's upward Y axis to SVG's downward Y axis. No simplified redraw or substitute icon is used.

- `brew.svg`: espresso cup and saucer
- `steam.svg`: steam jets and cloud
- `water.svg`: drop and two waves

The same paths are embedded in `index.html` as the shared `i-cup`, `i-steam`, and `i-drop` symbols. Color follows the skin's text color.
